Rules (fixed)

Rules change only through Claude, and only as a new version scored separately. No stop, no target, no adding: the backtest edge was measured hold-to-next-close, so that is what gets tested.

Runs hourly in market hours on GitHub Actions with Yahoo Finance bars (replay rows through Nov 12 used Webull bars). The trade log lives in this repo's docs/trades.json; weekly levels are set in overnight/config.json. A signal uses only hourly bars that have closed and enters at the next bar's open; 2 bps cost each side. "Benchmark" is buying the same ticker at 10:30 on every gate-open day and holding to the next close. A signal has to beat that, not zero. Level alerts copy Muse's four-part check on hourly bars (level, pressing, heavy volume, trend gate), enter at the next bar's open and exit at the same day's close; their benchmark is the 10:30-to-close move, flipped for shorts. The short side has no backtest support. Option prices are Black-Scholes estimates (no real quotes, no IV change), so treat them as rough. Replay rows were computed after the fact. Fewer than 30 trades means nothing yet. A backtest or paper result is not an expected return. Not trading or financial advice.
